SUMMA — CHROME EXTENSION (ready to load, nothing to build)
==========================================================

1. Open chrome://extensions  (or edge://extensions)
2. Turn ON "Developer mode" (toggle, top right)
3. Click "Load unpacked"
4. Select THIS folder — the one containing manifest.json
5. Pin the Summa icon to your toolbar

The teal Summarize button now appears AUTOMATICALLY on every page you
visit — click it to scan. The toolbar icon does the same, and toggles
the panel on pages you've already opened it on. The scanner reads the
ENTIRE page and shows 5 numbered main points plus a full
non-redundant summary in a floating panel. At the top of the panel:
  - "Entire page / Article only" toggles how much of the page is read.
  - The content-type dropdown (Auto, Article, Email, Image-heavy,
    Documentation, Social/thread) tunes the scanner: emails weight the
    closing ask and action words; image-heavy pages pull in alt text
    and captions; docs keep more supporting detail.
  - "From URL" lets you paste ANY link and summarize that page without
    visiting it — the extension fetches and scans it directly.

Copy, rescan, drag, or close it.

Works on Gmail, GitHub, X, YouTube — anywhere a bookmarklet gets blocked —
because extensions are exempt from page CSP rules.

Everything runs locally. Nothing is sent to any server.
